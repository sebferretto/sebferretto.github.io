Hobbies & Interests

Besides making and playing video games, here are a few things I share a similar passion for.

Skateboarding

Learning to ollie got me obsessed with the physics behind skateboard tricks. The sense of accomplishment after landing something you've tried for hours — or weeks — is unlike anything else. Skateboarding has had a real impact on my discipline and confidence, and it's taught me to get back up when I fall. I'd love to work on a skateboarding game that captures that feeling someday.

Music

Music has always been a big part of my life — whether I'm at a concert, watching skate videos, playing a video game, or getting lost in an album. I find that the video games that stick with me most tend to do so because of their music and atmosphere. I play guitar as well, and while I don't have much experience recording music, I'm looking forward to eventually making music for my own games.

Camping

As much as I like air conditioning and being cozy, I do enjoy spending time outdoors. My friends and I try to go back-country camping each summer in Algonquin Park, which is something I look forward to all year. Taking time away from the noise of the city (and the internet) helps me come back to everyday life with a fresh perspective.
